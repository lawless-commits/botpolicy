Example Co

Example Co makes plain, durable kitchen tools for people who cook every day. This page is served as ordinary HTML, so any crawler can read it without running a single script.

Our knives are forged from one piece of steel and sharpened by hand. Our pans are cast iron, seasoned before they ship, and come with a lifetime guarantee. We ship to every province.

Questions about an order, a return or a warranty claim go to our support team, who answer within one working day. Wholesale and press enquiries have their own pages.
